# Guition JC4827W543, 4.3 inch, 480 × 272 (experimental)

A 4.3-inch board from Guition (**JC4827W543C**): a 480 x 272 panel with an **NV3041A** driver on a four-line QSPI
bus and **GT911 capacitive touch** over I2C, on an ESP32-S3 with 4 MB flash and 8 MB octal PSRAM (N4R8). The same
board is sold as **JC4827W543R** with resistive touch and **JC4827W543N** without touch; the board file is for the
capacitive C only.

The board is **experimental**: its file uses ESPHome's own `mipi_spi` model for this board (`JC4827W543`), which
carries the panel's init sequence, size, colours and data rate, with the touch pins from the manufacturer's
documentation. It has been built and rendered, not yet tried on a real unit by the project. If you have one,
[tell others how it went](https://tessera-maxgramser.on-forge.com/community/share?type=installation&board=jc4827w543).

## Install

Update Tessera Screen Manager and choose **Guition · 4.3 inch** (JC4827W543) in **New screen**. Follow
[Easy setup](EASY_SETUP.md) to create a profile with its own name, Wi-Fi references and unique API/OTA keys, then
flash it over USB or download the firmware for ESPHome Web. There is no touch calibration: the touch panel reports
pixels.

The remote package is `packages/jc4827w543.yaml`; the checkout entry is `checkout/jc4827w543.yaml`. Both combine
`packages/core.yaml` with `packages/boards/guition-jc4827w543.yaml`, which shares the chip, the panel bus and the
backlight with the JC3248W535 through `packages/hardware/guition-esp32s3-qspi.yaml`.

A screen you set up by hand from the shared packages needs the board's `BOARD_ID` and a `DEVICE_NAME` equal to its
`esphome: name:`: Tessera knows a screen by the two, and a screen without them can be seen but never takes tiles.
Building from `packages/jc4827w543.yaml` sets both.

## Layout and capabilities

- Lying down: 480 x 272, two by two cells. Standing up: 272 x 480, one column of four.
- The compact look at 128 dpi (552 diagonal pixels over 4.3 inches), as on the CYD: the standard look's larger
  letters do not fit in 4 MB of flash.
- Capacitive touch on an interrupt line, read every 20 ms at most.
- The backlight is a PWM pin (GPIO1) at 1000 Hz: dimming, standby, night and an alert's flashes work.
- Camera pictures, live camera tiles and media artwork, from the PSRAM.
- 4 MB of flash: the wide update slots of [FLASH_LAYOUT.md](FLASH_LAYOUT.md), and the bedside clock draws with the
  display step instead of digits of its own, as on the CYD. The firmware takes about 93 % of its update slot.
- LVGL draws through a 25 % buffer. Read `sensor.<screen>_heap_free` with a full layout; under 40 KB is too little.

## Pins

| Part | Pins |
| --- | --- |
| QSPI bus (display) | CLK GPIO47, data GPIO21, GPIO48, GPIO40, GPIO39, CS GPIO45 |
| I2C bus (touch, GT911) | SDA GPIO8, SCL GPIO4, INT GPIO3, RST GPIO38 |
| Backlight | GPIO1 |

The board also has a speaker connector and a microSD slot. The board file leaves both out.

## Still to check

- Touch in all four corners and a drag, lying down and standing up. A screen whose taps land mirrored or swapped can
  set `TOUCH_MIRROR_X`, `TOUCH_MIRROR_Y` or `TOUCH_SWAP_XY` in its own YAML
  ([Hardware-specific YAML overrides](EASY_SETUP.md)).
- The colours and a page switch: the panel bus runs at 20 MHz, ESPHome's rate for this model.
- The backlight's dimming at 1000 Hz, and the memory with a full layout and a camera tile.
